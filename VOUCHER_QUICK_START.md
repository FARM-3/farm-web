# Voucher System - Quick Start Guide

## What's New?

Your Rugyeyo web application now has an automatic voucher generation system! When someone submits a wage, they can download a professional PDF voucher with all payment details.

## How It Works

### Option 1: Download After Submission (Automatic)

1. Go to **Wage Entry** page ([WageEntry.jsx](src/pages/WageEntry.jsx))
2. Fill out and submit the wage form
3. After successful submission, a modal appears asking:
   - **"Download Voucher"** - Generates and downloads the PDF immediately
   - **"Skip for Now"** - Goes to wages page without downloading

### Option 2: Download from Wages List (Manual)

1. Go to **Wages** page ([wages.jsx](src/pages/wages.jsx))
2. Find any wage record in the table
3. Click the green **download icon** (📥) in the Actions column
4. Voucher PDF downloads automatically
5. Success message appears: "Voucher VCH-202511-000042 downloaded successfully!"

## Voucher Contents

Each voucher includes:

- **Voucher Number**: Unique ID (e.g., VCH-202511-000042)
- **Employee Information**: Name, Staff ID, Payment Date
- **Payment Breakdown**:
  - Days worked
  - Monthly salary
  - Deductions
  - Net amount paid (highlighted)
- **Notes**: Reason for payment
- **Signature Areas**: For employee and authorized personnel
- **Official Footer**: Rugyeyo Enterprise branding
